Feed an adult cat a measured morning meal around 8:00. Cats often prefer several small meals, so you can add an evening meal if you already do that. Do not feed dog food as a regular diet. This note does not set a calorie amount.

task_description: Scoop litter box
keywords: litter, box, scoop, cat, waste, clean
---
Scoop the litter box at least once a day. A practical time is 10:00, after the morning meal. A box that is left dirty is a common reason cats stop using it. Sudden straining or blood in the box needs a veterinarian the same day.

task_description: Play session
keywords: play, toy, cat, adult, enrichment, hunt
---
Plan a short play session of about 15 minutes, often in the late afternoon around 16:00. Use a wand toy and let the cat catch it at the end. Put string and plastic bags away when you are done. This is enrichment, not medical treatment.

task_description: Quiet play session

---
A senior cat usually enjoys a quieter play session of about 10 minutes around 15:00. Keep jumps low and stop if the cat walks away. Mention mobility changes at the next veterinary visit.
