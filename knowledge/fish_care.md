Feed fish a small portion they can finish in a couple of minutes, commonly once in the morning around 8:30. Uneaten food clouds the water, so remove what is left. The right food depends on the species. This note is not a feeding chart for every aquarium.

---
Once a day, around 18:00, confirm the filter is running and the water is clear. Do not replace all of the water at once. A partial water change is a separate task you should only do on the schedule you already use for that tank. Gasping at the surface or a sudden temperature swing needs someone experienced with aquariums the same day.
