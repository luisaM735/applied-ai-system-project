A healthy adult dog usually needs a daily walk of about 30 minutes. A reliable morning slot is 7:30, before the household gets busy. Let the dog set a comfortable pace and stop if they limp, cough, or refuse to continue. This is general routine guidance, not a veterinary exam.

---
A senior dog often does better with a shorter gentle walk of about 20 minutes, commonly around 9:00 when the day is cooler. Watch for stiffness after the walk. Ask a veterinarian before increasing distance. This is general routine guidance, not a diagnosis.

Offer an adult dog a measured morning meal around 8:00. Many dogs also eat an evening meal; add that separately if your veterinarian has recommended twice-daily feeding. Keep human food and trash out of reach. This schedule does not set a calorie amount.

task_description: Refill water bowl

---
Check the water bowl by midday and refill it with fresh water. Wash the bowl if it is slimy. A dog that suddenly drinks far more or far less than usual should be seen by a veterinarian. Do not add medicines to the water.
