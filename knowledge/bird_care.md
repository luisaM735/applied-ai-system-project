Refresh a bird's food and water in the morning, around 8:00. Remove spoiled fresh food the same day. The right mix of pellets, vegetables, and seed depends on the species, so confirm the diet with an avian veterinarian. Do not share avocado, chocolate, or caffeine.

---
Tidy the cage daily around 11:00. Change soiled papers and wipe perches that are visibly dirty. A full disinfectant clean can be weekly. If the bird is fluffed up and quiet while you clean, give them space and call an avian veterinarian if that posture continues.
